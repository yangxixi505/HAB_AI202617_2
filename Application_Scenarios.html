Who We Serve

1

Coastal Management Agencies

State and local agencies use our UAV system for early detection and targeted response. Instead of waiting days for lab results, teams can deploy a drone and receive actionable data within hours, enabling faster advisories and more precise closures.

Tourism-Dependent Municipalities

Beach towns rely on accurate, localized monitoring to protect public health without unnecessary beach closures. Our mobile system allows proactive shoreline monitoring, helping communities reduce economic losses while maintaining public trust.

Aquaculture & Fisheries Operators

Shellfish farms and fisheries use our technology to protect stock before contamination occurs. Mobile detection enables operators to track bloom movement and act early, avoiding costly shutdowns and product losses.

Marine Research Institutions

Research labs and universities use the system as a flexible data collection platform, with customizable flight paths and AI models trained on local waters to support long-term ecological research.
