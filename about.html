About US



We are a student-led team driven by a shared passion for STEM and environmental protection. After witnessing the increasing impact of marine red tides on ecosystems, fisheries, and coastal communities, we identified a critical limitation in existing monitoring systems: they respond too late to prevent damage.

By integrating engineering, artificial intelligence, and environmental science, we developed a UAV-based intelligent monitoring system that enables faster, more accessible, and predictive red tide detection. Our goal is to make advanced environmental monitoring both affordable and practical


Our Mission What do we do now



Our mission is to provide fast, automated, and predictive red tide monitoring using UAV and AI technology.

We focus on transforming slow, labor-intensive, and reactive monitoring into a system that delivers near–real-time data and early warnings, enabling users to take action before ecological and economic damage occurs.


Our Vision What we aim to change long-term



Our vision is a world where marine disasters are prevented, not merely responded to. We envision intelligent monitoring becoming a standard tool for protecting the environment by supporting sustainable aquaculture, preserving biodiversity, and empowering coastal communities with accessible, data-driven environmental intelligence.
